Welcome to HSIA

A warm welcome to the heart of Bangladesh.

Located in Kurmitola, just 17 kilometers from Dhaka's vibrant center, Hazrat Shahjalal International Airport has evolved with Bangladesh since 1980.

With an annual passenger handling capacity of 12.5 million, our dedicated professional team is available 24/7 to make your journey smooth, safe and memorable.
